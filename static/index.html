How well does this site protect its visitors?

Paste a web address. SecureCheck reads what the site already shows every browser and grades it from A+ to F, with the fix for each problem.

https://

What gets checked

Encryption
TLS versions offered, forward secrecy, certificate trust, expiry and key strength, and whether plain HTTP redirects to HTTPS.
Browser protections
HSTS, Content Security Policy, clickjacking defence, CORS, and whether the server leaks its software version.
Cookies
Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite on every cookie the page sets.
Page content
Third-party scripts without integrity hashes, mixed content, and outdated jQuery, Bootstrap or AngularJS.
Domain and email
SPF and DMARC against email spoofing, DNSSEC, CAA records, MTA-STS and a security.txt contact.

Each finding is mapped to the related controls in ISO 27001, SOC 2, PCI DSS and the OWASP Top 10.

Every check is passive. The scanner sends a handful of ordinary requests, the same ones a browser sends, and never attempts to break in.

    Scan only sites you own or are curious about as a visitor. Results describe public configuration at the moment of the scan and are not a penetration test.
